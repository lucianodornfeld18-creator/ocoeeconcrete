Central Florida Hardscape Conditions

Central Florida's sand-clay soil mix and high water table require a 6–8 inch crushed stone base (vs the 4 inch standard in northern states) and a 1% minimum drainage slope. Improper base prep is the #1 cause of paver failure in Orange, Osceola, Lake and Polk counties. Every install we do uses 95%+ Standard Proctor compaction, woven geotextile fabric, and rigid edge restraints to handle Florida's seasonal water cycles.

Our Process

Our 38-Point Installation Process

The same 38-point process on every paver and concrete project, from Ocoee to Lake Nona. No shortcuts, no skipped steps.

🔍

Site Assessment & Planning

8 points

  1. On-site measurement with laser-grade tools
  2. Underground utility marking via Sunshine 811
  3. Soil composition and drainage assessment
  4. HOA / ARB requirements verification
  5. Permit research (Orange / Osceola / Lake / Polk county)
  6. Drainage slope calculation (minimum 1% pitch)
  7. Existing surface demolition plan
  8. Written scope with material selection & timeline
⛏️

Excavation & Base Preparation

10 points

  1. Excavate 8–10 inches below finished grade
  2. Remove all topsoil, organic matter and roots
  3. Install woven geotextile fabric on subgrade
  4. Lay 6–8 inches of compacted #57 crushed stone base
  5. Compact base in 2-inch lifts (plate compactor)
  6. Verify base compaction (95%+ Standard Proctor)
  7. Install rigid PVC or steel edge restraints
  8. Lay 1-inch concrete sand setting bed
  9. Screed sand to perfectly flat plane
  10. Final grade check across full installation area
🧱

Installation

10 points

  1. Place pavers in specified pattern (running bond, herringbone, etc.)
  2. Maintain consistent 3mm joint spacing
  3. Cut perimeter pavers with wet saw for clean edges
  4. Verify pattern alignment every 10 linear feet
  5. Set custom inlays or banding where designed
  6. Final placement check against scope drawings
  7. Sweep polymeric joint sand into all joints
  8. Activate polymeric sand with light water mist
  9. Plate-compact entire installation (with rubber mat)
  10. Repeat polymeric sand sweep + activation
✅

Finishing & Quality Control

6 points

  1. Verify final elevation against driveway / pool deck transitions
  2. Pressure-test drainage slope with water flow check
  3. Inspect every joint for full polymeric fill
  4. Touch up any displaced pavers or sand
  5. Apply optional sealant (clear, wet-look, or color-enhance)
  6. Final pattern + edge alignment QC walkthrough
🧹

Cleanup & Documentation

4 points

  1. Remove all construction debris from site
  2. Pressure-wash adjacent surfaces (driveway aprons, sidewalks)
  3. Document the completed install with full photo set
  4. Provide written 2-year workmanship warranty paperwork

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about paver, concrete and hardscape work in Central Florida.

How much does a paver driveway cost in Central Florida? +

Paver driveways across Central Florida typically range from $8,000 to $22,000, depending on size, paver style and site conditions. The biggest cost drivers are square footage, paver brand (Belgard / Tremron / Pavestone), and whether existing concrete needs to be demolished first. A 600 sq ft driveway with mid-grade concrete pavers usually runs $10,000–$14,000 installed. Call (689) 243-5726 for a free on-site estimate.

How is your installation process different from other contractors? +

We follow a written 38-point installation process on every job — from on-site soil assessment and 95% Standard Proctor base compaction, to woven geotextile fabric, polymeric sand activation, and final QC walkthrough. Most local contractors skip the geotextile or under-compact the base. That's why so many pavers in Central Florida sink or shift after 2–3 years. See all 38 points →

Which Central Florida cities do you serve? +

We serve a 30-mile radius of Ocoee, FL — that includes Ocoee, Winter Garden, Orlando, Kissimmee, Windermere, Apopka, Clermont, Davenport, Lake Nona, Dr. Phillips, Four Corners and Celebration. See all paver service areas or all concrete service areas.

Are pavers better than concrete for Florida's climate? +

Both work well — pavers handle Florida's heat, rain and occasional freeze-thaw better than poured slabs because they flex naturally, drain through joints, and individual pavers can be replaced without breaking up the whole surface. Concrete is more affordable upfront and great for utility surfaces. We install both and recommend based on your goals and budget. Read our full comparison →

Do you offer financing? +

We don't offer in-house financing, but most homeowners use home improvement loans (Synchrony, GreenSky), HELOCs, or contractor card programs through our recommended lenders. We can connect you to financing partners during the estimate process.

How fast can you start a paver or concrete project? +

From signed quote to project start is typically 1–3 weeks during the busy season (Feb–May, Sep–Nov) and 1 week during slower months (June–Aug, Dec–Jan). Smaller jobs (patios, walkways) can sometimes start within a week. Permitting and HOA approval can add 1–4 weeks depending on the city.

Do you handle permits and HOA approval? +

Yes. We handle Orange, Osceola, Lake and Polk county permits as part of our service and include permit fees in our quote. For HOA-managed communities (Hamlin, Watermark, Heritage Hills, Celebration, etc.) we provide a complete submission packet with material samples, dimensioned drawings and color renderings.

What warranty do you provide? +

Every project comes with a written 2-year workmanship warranty, transferable to the next property owner. On top of that, all Belgard and Tremron pavers carry the manufacturer's lifetime warranty against structural failure. Read the full warranty →
