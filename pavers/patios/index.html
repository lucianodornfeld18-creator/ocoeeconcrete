Patio Design

Paver patios for Central Florida outdoor living

Central Florida's climate gives you 9-10 months of comfortable outdoor living per year — but only if you have the right surface to use. A 12x16 concrete slab is functional but it's not where you want to spend a Saturday evening. A 14x20 paver patio with the right pattern, color, and integration into the landscape is.

We design and install paver patios in three primary categories: standalone backyard entertaining spaces, lanai expansions (extending an existing covered porch into the yard), and pool-deck-adjacent patios that tie into the pool area without crossing into the pool deck itself.

What size patio should you build?

For a small outdoor dining set (4 chairs + small table): 10x12 minimum, 12x14 ideal. For a full outdoor living layout (sectional + dining + fire pit area): 16x20 minimum, 20x24 ideal. For elaborate setups with outdoor kitchens and multiple zones: 24x28+ with proper zoning.

Patterns and what they signal

Herringbone is the traditional choice — durable, classic, slightly formal. Running bond reads more modern and simpler. Basketweave is rare but works well with smaller paver units in cottage-style homes. Random ashlar (mixed paver sizes) reads upscale and works well with larger patios. We'll show samples on-site.

Tying into existing hardscape

Most Central Florida patios connect to either an existing slab (concrete lanai) or a paver walkway. We use proper expansion joints at the connection point and color-match or contrast-coordinate the new pattern with the existing surface.

Construction Standards

How we build paver patios

Patio installation follows the same base-prep discipline as driveway work, with two important differences: drainage and traffic load.

Drainage matters more on patios

Driveways have natural slope away from the house (1-2% standard). Patios are often surrounded by lawn, walls, or planted beds that can trap water. We design proper slope (1-1.5%) away from any adjacent structure, integrate French drains or surface drains where needed, and verify drainage with a water test before completing the polymeric sand step.

Load specifications are lighter

Patio loads are pedestrian-only — no vehicles, no trucks. We can use 5-6 inches of base instead of 8 (saves cost without sacrificing longevity), and lighter-duty edge restraint. Same paver thickness (60mm standard residential pavers work for both).

Material selection for patios specifically

For patios where you'll be walking barefoot in summer, we recommend lighter-colored pavers (silver, ivory, light gray) over dark charcoal or chocolate tones — surface temperature can be 20-30°F different. For poolside areas, travertine or textured pavers are preferred for slip resistance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Paver Patio Installation FAQ

How much does a paver patio cost?

Small (10x12) patios in standard pavers run $1,800-$2,800. Medium (14x18) patios: $3,500-$5,500. Large (18x24): $6,000-$9,500. Premium materials and add-ons (lighting, fire pits, outdoor kitchen prep) increase pricing accordingly.

How long does paver patio installation take?

A typical 250-400 sq ft patio installs in 2-3 working days. Larger patios with complex patterns or grade work: 3-5 days. Patio + walkway combinations: 4-6 days.

Do I need a permit for a backyard paver patio?

Most patios in detached backyards under 200 sq ft don't require a permit in our service area. Larger patios, patios attached to the home, or patios that involve drainage work into a stormwater system typically do require a permit. We confirm during the estimate.

Can you build the patio around my existing pool?

Yes — we design and install paver patios that integrate with existing pool decks. We coordinate around the pool tile/coping, ensure proper expansion joints between paver and existing concrete, and color-coordinate the materials.

What about outdoor kitchens, fire pits, and built-in seating?

We do the patio foundation and rough-in for water, gas, and electrical. We coordinate with licensed plumbers, electricians, and outdoor kitchen specialists for finished installations. Many customers handle the appliance selection themselves and we coordinate the install timing.

Will weeds grow through my paver patio?

If polymeric joint sand is properly activated, no. Polymeric sand creates a flexible but solid joint that weeds can't establish in. If your patio is in a heavily shaded area, occasional weeding may still be needed at joint edges where moss can grow. Annual maintenance is minimal.
