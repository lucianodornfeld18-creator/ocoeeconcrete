Wall Types

Retaining wall types we install

Retaining walls hold back soil where there’s a grade change — a sloped backyard that needs leveling, a driveway cut into a hill, or a pool deck that needs to step down to a lower yard. Central Florida’s sandy soils make most walls structurally simpler than rocky terrain, but drainage behind the wall is critical to prevent water pressure failures.

Segmental block retaining walls

Modular concrete block units (Belgard, Tremron) stacked in a running bond pattern. Each course steps back slightly to lean into the retained soil. Great for landscape walls under 4 feet high. $25–$55 per sq ft of wall face.

Poured concrete walls

Cast-in-place concrete walls with rebar reinforcement, used for taller walls (4+ feet) or where high load capacity is required. $45–$80 per sq ft of wall face.

Boulder / natural stone walls

For rustic landscape design where the wall is the visual focal point. Material cost varies widely; install runs $30–$60 per sq ft.

Engineering and permits

Walls over 4 feet tall in Central Florida typically require engineered plans and a permit. We coordinate with structural engineers and handle the permit process for engineered walls.

💧Drainage is non-negotiableEvery retaining wall we build includes proper drainage behind the wall: 3/4-inch clean gravel backfill, perforated drainpipe at the base, and weep holes through the wall for hydrostatic pressure relief. Wall failures are almost always drainage failures.
FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need a permit for a retaining wall?

In most Central Florida jurisdictions, retaining walls under 4 feet tall don’t require a permit. Walls 4 feet and taller typically require engineered plans and a building permit.

How long does a segmental block retaining wall last?

With proper base prep, drainage behind the wall, and quality block (Belgard, Tremron), a segmental block wall lasts 30–50 years in Central Florida.

Can you build a retaining wall around my pool deck?

Yes — retaining walls around pool decks are common when the pool deck needs to be raised relative to surrounding grade. We coordinate with the pool deck installation to ensure proper expansion joints and drainage.
