What Happens Next

Our response timeline

Here's exactly what happens after you contact us:

  1. Initial response (within 2 hours during business hours). We confirm we received your request and ask any clarifying questions about your project.
  2. On-site estimate scheduled (within 1-3 days). We come to your property at a time that works for you, including evenings and weekends. Most estimates take 20-40 minutes on-site.
  3. Written estimate sent (within 48 hours of on-site visit). You get an itemized PDF estimate via email with line-item pricing, material specs, timeline, permit costs, and warranty terms.
  4. Material samples (if requested). If you're choosing between paver colors or concrete finishes, we drop off physical samples for in-person review.
  5. Project scheduled (after you approve). Once you approve the estimate, we handle permits and HOA approval. Installation typically starts 2-4 weeks from approval.

If you don't hear from us within the expected window, please call us directly at (689) 243-5726 — occasionally email forms get filtered into spam folders.
