What about pavers over an existing concrete driveway in Winter Garden?

Installing pavers over existing concrete (called “overlay”) is technically possible and saves on demolition, but it raises the finished height by 2–2.5 inches — which usually means problems at the garage threshold and the street curb transition. We rarely recommend it on a primary driveway in Winter Garden. For a patio or pool deck where the height change is workable, overlay can save $2–$3 per sq ft.

Paver patio pricing in Winter Garden

A paver patio is the most common entry-level project for Winter Garden homeowners testing the waters with hardscape. A typical 300 sq ft backyard paver patio in Winter Garden runs $5,400–$8,400 installed.

The variables that move that number:

  • Shape complexity. A simple rectangle is the cheapest. Curved edges, multiple levels, or built-in fire pit areas add 15–30%.
  • Paver size. 4x8 Holland is the cheapest. Large-format planks (12x24, 6x18) cost 20–40% more per sq ft.
  • Edge type. A standard PVC edge restraint hidden by sod is the default. A decorative soldier course (perpendicular border) adds $4–$6 per linear foot.
  • Existing surface. Bare grass is the easiest (no demo). Existing concrete adds $2–$4 per sq ft in removal cost.

Pool deck pavers in Winter Garden

Pool deck pavers run $15–$22 per sq ft installed in Winter Garden for concrete pavers and $22–$30 per sq ft for travertine. Travertine dominates the Winter Garden pool deck market for one good reason: it stays measurably cooler than concrete pavers in Florida sun — typically 15–20°F lower surface temperature at noon. For barefoot pool use, that matters a lot.

Pool decks have specific code requirements in Central Florida: slip-resistance ratings, drainage away from the pool, and proper expansion joints around the pool coping. Our pool deck service page covers the technical requirements in detail.

Paver walkway pricing in Winter Garden

Walkways are the smallest paver projects and have the highest per-sq-ft cost because of fixed setup labor (mobilization, base prep equipment, polymeric sand activation gear) is spread over fewer square feet. A 100 sq ft walkway in Winter Garden typically costs $2,400–$4,200.

What pushes paver pricing up or down in Winter Garden

The same 600 sq ft driveway can quote at $10,000 or $18,000 depending on the property. Here are the main variables specific to Winter Garden and Central Florida:

  1. Property access. If the work area is reachable from the street with a wheelbarrow, costs stay low. If material has to be hauled through a side gate, around a pool, or up steps, add 8–15%.
  2. Existing surface removal. Demolishing and hauling out 4 inches of existing concrete adds $1.50–$3.50 per sq ft. Asphalt demolition costs roughly the same.
  3. Soil conditions. Most Winter Garden properties have sandy or sandy-clay soils that excavate cleanly. Properties near Plant Street Market occasionally hit organic muck layers that need full removal and replacement — add $400–$1,200.

  5. Paver brand and color. Stock colors (gray, sand, charcoal) are warehouse-stocked. Specialty colors require special order and can add $2–$4 per sq ft plus 7–10 day lead time.

Pavers vs concrete in Winter Garden

The cheap answer is that pavers cost 1.5–2x more than poured concrete. The accurate answer is that pavers typically win on 25-year total cost of ownership.

A 600 sq ft poured concrete driveway in Winter Garden runs around $6,500. The same driveway in mid-grade pavers runs around $11,500 — a $5,000 premium.

What pavers buy you for that $5,000:

  • No visible cracks (when concrete cracks, the crack is permanent unless you replace the whole slab)
  • Individual stone replacement (a damaged paver costs $30–$80 to replace; a damaged concrete slab section requires $1,000–$2,500 cut-and-pour)
  • Higher resale impact — paver driveways are documented to add 1.5–2% to Winter Garden home appraisal value vs concrete in comparable comps
  • Better drainage (water passes through paver joints instead of pooling on a slab)

HOA and permit considerations in Winter Garden

Winter Garden is in Orange County and most driveway, patio or pool deck replacements require a building permit from the City of Winter Garden Building Department. Permit fees typically run $200–$450 depending on project value.

HOA approval is a separate process from the city permit. In Winter Garden, neighborhoods like Hamlin, Watermark, Stoneybrook West, Independence, Orchard Hills have active architectural review committees that require:

  • Paver brand and product line specification
  • Color sample for ARC files
  • Pattern and layout drawing
  • Edge detail and transition specs

We submit a complete ARC packet as part of every Winter Garden project that has an HOA — typically approved within 2–3 weeks.

How to save money on pavers in Winter Garden without cutting corners

If your budget is tight, here are the variables to optimize without sacrificing the things that matter (base prep, drainage, edge restraints):

  1. Choose a stock paver size and color. 4x8 Holland in gray, charcoal or sand is the cheapest combo — and looks great. Specialty sizes (12x24, multi-piece kits) cost 25–40% more.
  2. Skip the soldier course border. A border in a contrasting color looks great but adds 8–15% to the project cost. A simple flush sand edge with sod behind it looks clean too.
  3. Pick a basic pattern. Running bond and 90° herringbone are the standards — cheapest because they minimize cuts. Pinwheel and circle kits double the cuts and double the waste.
  4. Bundle multiple areas. Doing driveway + walkway + back patio in the same week saves on mobilization (one delivery, one crew setup) vs three separate visits. Typically saves 8–12%.
  5. Time of year matters less than you think. Some contractors offer summer discounts; we typically don’t because we run a full schedule year-round. But if you have flexibility on start date, ask.

Paver Cost FAQ — Winter Garden, FL

How much does a paver driveway cost in Winter Garden?

In Winter Garden, paver driveways typically run $8,000 to $22,000 installed. A standard 600 sq ft single-car driveway with mid-grade concrete pavers comes in around $10,000–$14,000. Travertine driveways start at $14,000.

Are pavers cheaper than concrete?

No — pavers cost roughly 1.5–2x more than poured concrete upfront. But over 25 years, pavers usually win on total cost of ownership because they don’t crack visibly, individual stones can be replaced, and they don’t need full demolition to repair.

How long does paver installation take in Winter Garden?

A typical Winter Garden paver driveway runs 3–5 days from start to finish: 1 day demo and excavation, 1–2 days base prep and compaction, 1–2 days laying and cutting, then polymeric sand and final compaction.

What’s the best paver brand for Florida?

For Central Florida, Belgard and Tremron dominate the residential market. Both are Florida-manufactured (lower transport cost, fresher product), carry lifetime structural warranties, and stock UV-stable colors that don’t fade in our climate.

Do you need a permit for pavers in Orange County?

Yes — in Winter Garden and most Central Florida cities, replacing an existing driveway or installing new hardscape over 100 sq ft typically requires a building permit. The contractor handles the application and you pay the fee through your project quote.

What’s the cheapest way to install pavers in Winter Garden?

Three ways to lower cost without sacrificing quality: (1) choose a standard size paver (Holland or rectangle, not custom shapes), (2) pick a popular color (gray, charcoal, sand — not specialty), (3) avoid intricate patterns like pinwheel that require more cuts and waste.
