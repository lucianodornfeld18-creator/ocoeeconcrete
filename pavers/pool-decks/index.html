Why Pavers for Pool Decks

Why pavers are the right pool deck surface in Florida

Pool deck pavers solve four problems that plague concrete pool decks in Florida: surface temperature, slip resistance, drainage, and crack management. Each of these is meaningful on a pool deck — they're the difference between a deck you actively use and one you tiptoe across.

Surface temperature is the biggest one. Plain concrete in summer Florida sun runs 130-150°F on a sunny afternoon — uncomfortable to walk on barefoot, painful for kids. Travertine runs 105-115°F under the same conditions. Light-colored concrete pavers run 110-125°F. The difference is hours of daily comfort.

Material options compared

Travertine (most popular): natural stone, premium look, cool surface, premium price. Concrete pavers: more affordable, wider design options, cool-touch versions available. Tumbled travertine: ages beautifully, very natural look. Pool-deck-specific lines from Belgard (HydraPressed) and Tremron (Pool Coping series): engineered specifically for pool environments.

Slip resistance is non-negotiable

Pool deck pavers need slip resistance that works when wet. We use textured surfaces (tumbled, chiseled, or sand-finished) and verify with the SCOF (Static Coefficient of Friction) rating that the paver meets ANSI A137.1 standards for wet conditions. Polished travertine — beautiful but slippery when wet — is not appropriate for pool decks.

Drainage prevents pool problems

A poorly drained pool deck dumps water back into the pool (high chemical use, water clarity issues) or pools standing water (mosquito breeding, algae growth, slip hazards). We design pool decks with 1-2% slope away from the pool coping toward strategically placed drains or natural runoff. Pool deck drainage is the most underrated detail in installations.

Crack-free for the deck's life

Pool decks crack frequently when poured concrete because of pool wall settlement, pool deck thermal expansion, and any soil movement. Pavers don't crack — they flex. Even if there's minor settlement, individual pavers can be lifted and reset without the deck showing damage.

Coping & Pool Edge

Pool coping options

Pool coping is the trim piece that caps the edge of the pool where the water meets the deck. It needs to be safe (no sharp edges), durable (constant water exposure), and visually coordinated with both the pool tile and the deck pavers.

Bull-nose travertine coping

The most popular choice. Travertine cut to a smooth, rounded bull-nose edge. Comes in matching colors to most travertine deck pavers (walnut, ivory, silver, noche). Pricing: $80-$150 per linear foot installed.

Concrete paver coping

Belgard, Tremron, and other manufacturers offer matching coping pieces for their paver lines. Less expensive than travertine, wider color options, but slightly less premium look. Pricing: $60-$100 per linear foot installed.

Custom-cut concrete coping

We can cast custom coping for unusual pool shapes or where matching specific colors is required. Pricing: $90-$140 per linear foot installed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Pool Deck Paver Installation FAQ

How much does a paver pool deck cost in Central Florida?

Small pool decks (300-450 sq ft): $4,800-$7,500 in concrete pavers or $6,500-$10,000 in travertine. Medium pool decks (500-750 sq ft): $8,000-$12,500 in concrete pavers or $11,000-$16,500 in travertine. Larger or custom installations price proportionally.

How much cooler is travertine than concrete in the sun?

Independent measurements show travertine running 15-25°F cooler than plain concrete in summer Florida sun. The exact difference depends on color (lighter is cooler) and surface texture, but the practical difference is going from "can't walk barefoot" to "comfortable on bare feet."

Can you install pool deck pavers around my existing pool?

Yes — most pool deck installations are around existing pools. We remove the existing concrete deck, install proper base prep with drainage, then install the new paver deck and matching coping. The pool itself isn't disturbed.

How long does pool deck paver installation take?

Typical 500-800 sq ft pool deck: 4-7 working days. Smaller decks: 3-4 days. Larger decks with complex shapes or accent banding: 7-10 days.

Is travertine slippery when wet?

Tumbled or chiseled travertine has very good slip resistance even when wet. Polished travertine — which is more decorative — is too slippery for pool deck use and not recommended. We only install textured travertine for pool deck applications.

Will pool chemicals damage the pavers?

Properly sealed travertine and quality concrete pavers handle pool chemicals well. We recommend sealing pool deck pavers with a penetrating sealer to enhance stain resistance to chlorine, sunscreen, food spills, and other typical pool deck exposures.

Do you handle pool resurfacing as well?

We don't do pool interior resurfacing (plaster, pebble, or aggregate finishes) — that's a different specialty. But we coordinate with pool resurfacing contractors to schedule the deck work and pool work together for efficiency.
