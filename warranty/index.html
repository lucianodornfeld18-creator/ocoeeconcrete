Our Warranty Coverage

Two layers of written protection on every job: our 2-year workmanship warranty plus the lifetime manufacturer warranty on Belgard and Tremron pavers.

Two Layers of Coverage

What we warrant, in plain English

Two pieces of paper come with every Ocoee Concrete & Pavers installation: our written 2-year workmanship warranty (issued and signed at project completion) and the manufacturer’s lifetime warranty on the pavers themselves. Together they cover essentially every realistic failure mode for a residential paver or concrete installation.

📜

2-Year Workmanship Warranty

From Ocoee Concrete & Pavers

Covers settlement, joint sand failure, edge restraint failure, drainage problems, and any installation defect for 2 years from completion date. Transferable to next property owner.

🏆

Lifetime Manufacturer Warranty

From Belgard / Tremron

Covers structural failure of the paver itself (cracking under normal load, manufacturing defects) for the lifetime of the original installation.

What our 2-year workmanship warranty covers

  • Base settlement. If the base settles enough to create a visible depression (more than 3/8 inch deflection from flat), we lift the affected pavers, recompact, and reset — at no charge.
  • Polymeric joint sand failure. If polymeric sand washes out, cracks, or fails to harden properly, we re-apply at no charge.
  • Edge restraint failure. If the PVC edge restraint shifts, breaks, or pulls away from spikes, we replace and re-secure at no charge.
  • Drainage issues. If standing water pools in an area where we installed proper slope, we re-grade at no charge.
  • Concrete cracking beyond normal hairlines. Hairline cracks (under 1/16 inch) are inherent to all concrete. Cracks wider than that within 2 years are covered.

What’s NOT covered (in plain English)

  • Acts of God: hurricanes, tropical storms, sinkholes, lightning damage
  • Vehicle impact: we’re not responsible if a truck backs over your pavers or a tree branch falls
  • Root damage: if you plant a tree near the installation and roots push pavers up, that’s a homeowner-side issue
  • Staining and color fade: manufacturer paver colors are UV-resistant but not UV-proof; some fade over decades
  • Improper sealer: if you (or another contractor) apply a sealer that traps moisture or reacts with polymeric sand, that voids the workmanship warranty
  • De-icer damage: rare in Florida, but rock salt and chemical de-icers damage concrete and pavers and are not covered

How the warranty actually works

If you see an issue:

  1. Email a photo to ocoeeconcreteflorida@gmail.com or send via WhatsApp at (689) 243-5726
  2. We’ll schedule an inspection within 5 business days
  3. If the issue is covered, repair work is scheduled at no charge (typically within 2 weeks of inspection)
  4. If the issue is outside warranty coverage, we provide a written explanation and quote for paid repair if requested

What happens after year 2?

The written workmanship warranty ends, but the manufacturer paver warranty continues for the life of the installation. Beyond year 2, we still service our own customers at cost (no markup). Most post-warranty service calls in years 3–5 are minor — typically $150–$400 for polymeric sand refresh, edge restraint adjustment, or minor settlement repair.

✍️ The warranty is signed paper, not a verbal promise We issue the warranty certificate at project completion with the project number, customer name, scope of work, and signed countersign. Photos of the completed work are filed with the warranty record.
Warranty FAQ

Common Warranty Questions

Is the warranty transferable?

Yes — our 2-year workmanship warranty transfers to the next owner if you sell the property within the warranty period. We simply re-issue the certificate in the new owner’s name.

What does the manufacturer lifetime warranty cover?

Belgard and Tremron lifetime warranties cover structural failure of the paver itself (the unit cracking under normal load). They don’t cover staining, color variation or settlement — those are workmanship issues covered by our 2-year warranty.

How do I file a warranty claim?

Call (689) 243-5726 or email ocoeeconcreteflorida@gmail.com with a photo of the issue and we’ll schedule an inspection within 5 business days. Most legitimate warranty claims are resolved with a follow-up service visit at no charge.

Does the warranty cover acts of God?

No. Tropical storms, hurricanes, sinkholes, vehicle impact damage, and tree root intrusion are not covered. Our warranty covers workmanship and material defects within normal use conditions.

What if my pavers settle in year 3?

Our written workmanship warranty is 2 years. Settlement after that is rare with our base prep, but if you do see settlement in years 3–5, we offer service-call repair at our cost (no markup). Most settlement issues are repairable for $200–$600.
