Paver Specialists Serving Orlando

Orlando is Central Florida's largest market and covers an enormous range of soil types — from sandy ridges in MetroWest to wetland-influenced clays near Lake Underhill and Lake Nona's reclaimed wetland substrate. We work primarily in West Orlando (Metrowest, Dr. Phillips, Williamsburg) and South Orlando (Hunter's Creek, Lake Nona), where new construction and 2000s-era driveways are due for replacement.

Our crews have installed paver driveways, patios, walkways and pool decks across Orlando — from Dr. Phillips and Metrowest on one side of town to Lake Eola Heights and Lake Fairview on the other. We pull current HOA design specs before quoting, source materials from regional Belgard and Tremron distributors, and follow a written 38-point installation process on every project.

🏗️ Central Florida Soil Context Central Florida’s clay-sand soil mix and high water table require a 6–8 inch crushed-limerock base (vs the 4” standard in northern states) plus woven geotextile fabric over the subgrade. Improper base prep is the #1 documented cause of paver settlement in Orange, Osceola, Lake and Polk counties. Every Ocoee Concrete & Pavers project includes 95% Standard Proctor compaction and a 1% drainage slope minimum.

Pavers in Orlando hold up to Florida’s summer heat, daily afternoon storms and occasional freeze-thaw cycles better than poured concrete because they flex naturally instead of cracking, drain water through joints rather than pooling, and let individual pavers be lifted and reset without affecting the entire surface. With a proper compacted limerock base and polymeric joint sand, quality concrete pavers in Orlando last 25–50 years.

We file all permits with the City of Orlando Permitting Services as part of the service and include the fees in your written quote. For HOA-governed communities in Orlando, we submit a complete design packet (material, color, pattern, edge detail) and coordinate the approval timeline before any excavation begins.

Our Process

The 38-Point Paver Installation Process

Every Orlando project follows the same documented 38-step sequence. This is what separates a 25-year installation from one that needs callbacks within 18 months.

🔍

Site Assessment & Planning

8 points

  1. On-site soil sample at 4 depths (4", 8", 12", 18")
  2. Underground utility marking (Sunshine 811 call placed 48h before dig)
  3. Drainage slope calculation (1% minimum away from structures)
  4. Existing grade and finish elevation measurement
  5. HOA design submission review (if applicable)
  6. Permit application filed with local building department
  7. Material quantity calculations with 8% waste factor
  8. Written project timeline with daily milestones
📏

Excavation & Base Preparation

8 points

  1. Excavate to depth: 8" residential driveway, 10" commercial
  2. Organic material and debris fully removed (no "capping")
  3. Subgrade compaction to 95% Standard Proctor
  4. Non-woven geotextile fabric installed full-width
  5. Crushed limerock base in 2" lifts, each compacted
  6. Plate compactor minimum 3 passes per lift
  7. Final base compaction verified with hand probe
  8. Bedding sand (ASTM C33 spec) at 1" depth, screeded flat
🧱

Installation

10 points

  1. Edge restraint installed on rigid PVC strip with 10" spikes
  2. First course laid against fixed edge with 1/8" joint
  3. Paver pattern locked: herringbone for driveways, running bond for walkways
  4. Cuts made with diamond blade saw — no chipping or hammer cuts
  5. Polymeric joint sand swept dry in 2 passes
  6. Joint sand vibrated in with plate compactor + rubber pad
  7. Polymeric sand activated with controlled mist (not flood) per ICPI
  8. Final compaction with rubber-mat plate compactor
  9. Surface protected for 24h cure before traffic
  10. Concrete: 4,000 psi mix minimum with fiber mesh reinforcement
✓

Finishing & Quality Control

6 points

  1. Slope verification with 4-foot level at 6 grid points
  2. Joint depth check (top of sand 1/8" below paver surface)
  3. Edge restraint hidden below grass line at all visible edges
  4. Color and pattern consistency walk-through (lead installer)
  5. Final cleaning with low-pressure rinse + manual sweep
  6. Optional sealer applied 30–60 days post-install
📂

Cleanup & Documentation

4 points

  1. All excavated soil, debris and packaging removed same-day
  2. Final photo documentation (before/during/after) emailed to homeowner
  3. Written 2-year workmanship warranty issued and signed
  4. Care guide for first 30 days handed to homeowner

Paver Questions — Orlando, FL

How much does a paver driveway cost in Orlando, FL?

In Orlando, paver driveways typically run $8,000–$22,000 installed. A 600 sq ft driveway with mid-grade concrete pavers (Belgard, Tremron) usually lands between $10,000 and $14,000. Travertine pricing starts around $14/sq ft installed. We provide free written estimates with no obligation — call (689) 243-5726.

Which Orlando neighborhoods do you serve?

We serve all of Orlando including Dr. Phillips, Metrowest, Lake Nona, Hunter's Creek, Lake Buena Vista, Audubon Park, and every other community within the Orlando city limits. Our crews work across all 27 Orlando ZIP codes.

Do I need a permit for pavers in Orlando?

For most paver driveway and patio replacements in Orlando, a permit is required from the City of Orlando Permitting Services. We handle the permit application as part of our service and include all fees in your written quote — no surprises.

How long does paver installation take in Orlando?

Most residential paver projects in Orlando are completed in 2–5 days. A typical 600 sq ft driveway runs 3–4 days from excavation to polymeric sand activation. Larger projects or complex patterns add 1–2 days.

How long do pavers last in Florida?

With proper 95% compacted limerock base, woven geotextile fabric and polymeric sand joints, quality concrete pavers in Florida last 25–50 years. Manufacturer warranties (Belgard, Tremron) are lifetime against structural failure. Re-sanding every 5–7 years extends life dramatically.

Will Orlando HOA approval be needed?

Many Orlando communities require HOA architectural review for driveway or patio changes. We submit a complete design packet with paver brand, color, pattern and edge detail — typically approved within 2–3 weeks. If your HOA is on our active approval list (most major Orlando HOAs are), we already know their requirements.

Are pavers better than concrete for Florida's climate?

Both work. Pavers handle Florida’s heat, rain and root pressure better because they flex naturally, drain through joints and individual units can be lifted and reset. Poured concrete is more affordable upfront but cracks are visible and harder to fix. For long-term ROI in Florida, pavers usually win.
